Daily Expense Tracker v1.7.1

New in v1.7.1:
- App name changed to Daily Expense Tracker.
- Added selectable multiple currency support.
- Currency applies to entry forms, dashboards, reports and exports.
- INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR, CAD, AUD, SGD, JPY, CNY and NPR included.

- Bill view export: PDF, Excel-compatible XLS and JPG
- Reports complete-bill export with every expense item
- Last six months Items Price Comparison report with average, lowest and highest rate
- Price Comparison export: PDF, Excel-compatible XLS and JPG
- Expense editing now includes item name, quantity, unit, rate and calculated amount
- Back from expense detail/edit returns to Transaction History
- Back from a shopping list returns to Shopping Lists
- Added expense items are shown at the top of Add Expense
- Item cards and item names use distinct theme colors
- Add New Item buttons use a separate tertiary color

Build note: Open the project in Android Studio, Sync, Clean Project and Rebuild Project.
